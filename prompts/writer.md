### WRITING RULES & CONSTRAINTS
1. TONE & STYLE: Professional, insightful, human-sounding, and grounded. Sound like an experienced senior engineer or technical founder writing candidly about systems and business reality.
2. NO CLICHÉS OR HYPE: Never use hype words ("game changer", "revolutionary", "10x in 2 clicks", "mind-blowing", "secret trick").
3. NO FAKE STATS OR CLAIMS: Do not invent statistics, fake percentages, or imaginary customer stories. If referencing concepts, discuss engineering principles, patterns, or real-world operational challenges.
4. HOOK: Must be captivating within the first 1-2 lines. It must grab attention by addressing an intellectual curiosity, a counter-intuitive observation, or a real engineering friction point. NO cheap clickbait.
5. BODY: 3 to 6 concise, readable paragraphs or bullet points. Use clean line breaks. Ensure strong pacing and high density of insight.
6. EMOJIS: Max 1-3 tasteful, functional emojis across the entire post (e.g. 📌, 💡, ↳). Never use emoji walls.
7. CALL TO ACTION (CTA): End with a thoughtful, open question inviting discussion from peers. Avoid aggressive self-promotion or hard sales pitches.
8. HASHTAGS: Provide 5 to 8 relevant, industry-standard hashtags (e.g. #SoftwareEngineering, #AI, #Automation, #CloudArchitecture).

Return the structured post according to schema:
- hook: The first 1-2 lines designed to stop the scroll.
- caption: The complete, ready-to-publish post text (including the hook at the start, body paragraphs, and call to action at the end).
- hashtags: List of 5-8 relevant hashtags including the '#' prefix.
- call_to_action: The concluding question or discussion starter.
