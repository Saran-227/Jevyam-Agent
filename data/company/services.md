# Official Service Pillars & Offerings: Jevyam Technologies

Jevyam Technologies delivers technology, digital solutions, and software engineering services across eight official pillars:

## 01. Inventory Management Systems
- **Core Focus**: Stock, warehouse, and supply chain control tailored to operational reality.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Stock and warehouse management across multiple locations.
  - Real-time purchase and sales tracking.
  - Raw material and finished goods reconciliation.
  - Scrap and wastage tracking.
  - Supplier catalogs, vendor logging, and purchase order lifecycles.
  - Automated low-stock alerts, reorder triggers, and executive management reporting.
- **Business Value**: Eliminates stockouts, curbs capital lockup in dead inventory, and eliminates manual spreadsheet reconciliation errors.

## 02. CRM Solutions
- **Core Focus**: Pipeline visibility, customer relationship nurturing, and sales operation velocity.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Centralized customer and vendor profile management.
  - Lead capture, tracking, qualification, and customized deal pipelines.
  - Quotations, proposal generation, and automated follow-up scheduling.
  - Order management and status workflows.
  - Sales and support workflow automation.
  - Complete interaction history, communications logging, and team task assignments.
- **Business Value**: Stops deals from falling through the cracks, increases closing velocity, and keeps sales teams aligned.

## 03. Custom Software Development
- **Core Focus**: Purpose-built software engineered around exact organizational workflows.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Custom business applications solving unique operational bottlenecks.
  - Scalable web and responsive desktop applications.
  - Local database engines, embedded storage, and robust data persistence.
  - Operational workflow automation scripts and data pipeline jobs.
  - API integrations and third-party SaaS connector development.
  - Maintainable, modular, and cleanly documented software architecture.
- **Business Value**: Provides competitive operational advantage over rigid off-the-shelf software packages.
