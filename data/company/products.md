# Products & Internal Solutions: Jevyam Technologies

The following products and modular platforms are proprietary technologies developed and maintained by Jevyam Technologies:

## 1. Jevyam Workflow Copilot
- **Category**: Intelligent Agent & Internal Operations Assistant.
- **Description**: An AI copilot engineered to integrate with company document stores, ticketing systems (Jira, Linear, Zendesk), and messaging platforms (Slack, Teams).
- **Key Capabilities**:
  - Context-aware triage of incoming support inquiries and internal tickets.
  - Automated drafting of resolutions based on company SOPs and past tickets.
  - Human-in-the-loop escalation workflows with complete audit logging.

## 2. Jevyam DataBridge
- **Category**: Intelligent Ingestion & RAG Readiness Pipeline.
- **Description**: A high-throughput data connector and transformation engine designed to extract unstructured business data (PDFs, spreadsheets, database exports, API payloads) and prepare it for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and semantic search.
- **Key Capabilities**:
  - Automated chunking, cleaning, metadata tagging, and vector embedding.
  - Built-in deduplication and schema normalization.
  - Connectors for Postgres, MongoDB, cloud object storage, and SaaS webhooks.
